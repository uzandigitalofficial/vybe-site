Community Guidelines

Keep the vybe good.

Be real

Use your real identity, host events you actually intend to run, and only reserve spots you plan to use.

Be safe

No harassment, hate speech, threats, doxxing, or sexual content. No events promoting illegal activity — including illegal street racing.

Be respectful at events

Respect venues, neighbours and other attendees. Hosts set the rules for their events.

Report & block

Every profile, event, post and message has a report option. Blocking someone hides all of your content from each other, permanently. Reports are reviewed and violations lead to content removal or account suspension.

Enforcement

First violation: content removed and a warning. Serious or repeat violations: permanent ban. Contact uzandigitalofficial@gmail.com to appeal.
