Delete your account

Duskaro, by UZAN Digital.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Duskaro and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email uzandigitalofficial@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject “Delete my Duskaro account”, and include your @username. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

Your profile, posts, stories, likes, comments, reservations, follows, messages and uploaded photos and videos. Nothing is kept afterwards, except where the law requires us to keep a record of a safety report you were part of — held for up to 90 days, then deleted.
